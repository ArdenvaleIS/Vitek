Recognize your issue

You do not need to know which technical service to request. Start with the symptoms or decision you are facing.

“My Wi-Fi keeps dropping or does not reach some rooms.”

I can check signal coverage, access-point placement, interference, configuration and network bottlenecks before you buy new equipment.

I can investigate moisture entry, air leakage, condensation, ventilation and insulation conditions. See Mold & Moisture services.

Central vacuum systems

Potential advantages

  • Strong suction performance. Many central vacuum units can provide higher suction or airflow than typical portable vacuums. Actual performance depends on the power unit, pipe layout, hose, filtration and maintenance.
  • Exhaust can be routed outdoors. When correctly installed with an outdoor exhaust, air leaving the central power unit is discharged outside the occupied rooms rather than blown back into the room being cleaned.

Outdoor exhaust reduces recirculation from the vacuum unit; it does not prevent the cleaning head from disturbing dust, and it is not a guarantee of allergy or health benefits.

I can help evaluate an existing central vacuum system or discuss design and performance considerations. I do not sell vacuum equipment or install systems.
