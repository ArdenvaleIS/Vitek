Recognize your issue

“My Wi-Fi keeps dropping or does not reach some rooms.”

Check signal coverage, placement, interference, configuration, and network bottlenecks before buying new equipment.

“My house has hot or cold spots, or the HVAC is noisy.”

Evaluate airflow, controls, registers, insulation, air leakage, and equipment settings.

“My electricity or natural gas bill seems too high.”

Look for avoidable energy use, operating inefficiencies, and cost-effective changes to existing systems.

“A contractor says I need an expensive upgrade.”

Get a second opinion on alternatives before replacing HVAC equipment or upgrading electrical panels or utility service.

“I have a solar PV, battery, or EV charging proposal.”

Review proposed system sizing, integration, operating assumptions, and load-management options.

“My computer, TV, phone, or connected devices are unreliable.”

Investigate configuration, software, performance, connectivity, or battery-life issues.
