For homeowners and individuals

Independent technical advice for your home.

Find the root cause. Avoid unnecessary costs.

Unreliable Wi-Fi, computers or connected devices? Uncomfortable rooms, moisture problems, high energy costs or an expensive contractor proposal?

I can install, configure and troubleshoot home IT and connected systems directly, assess building-performance issues, and provide independent advice before major equipment purchases or upgrades.

Recognize your issue

You do not need to know which technical service to request. Start with the symptoms or decision you are facing.

“My Wi-Fi keeps dropping or does not reach some rooms.”

I can diagnose coverage and network bottlenecks, then adjust Wi-Fi configuration, access points and connected equipment where appropriate—not automatically recommend a new router.

“My house has hot or cold spots, or the HVAC is noisy.”

I can evaluate airflow, controls, registers, insulation, air leakage and equipment settings.

“My electricity or natural gas bill seems too high.”

I can look for avoidable energy use, operating inefficiencies and cost-effective changes to existing systems.

“A contractor says I need an expensive upgrade.”

I can provide an independent second opinion and assess whether the proposed equipment is actually responsible for the problem.

“Mold or dampness keeps returning.”

I can investigate moisture entry, air leakage, condensation, ventilation and insulation conditions. See Mold & Moisture services.

“I have a solar PV, battery or EV-charging proposal.”

I can review proposed system sizing, integration, operating assumptions and load-management options.

“My computer, TV, phone or connected devices are unreliable.”

I can troubleshoot and configure computers, software, TVs, smartphones and connected devices, including hands-on setup and remote support when suitable.

Independent second opinion.

Already received a proposal from a contractor, installer or equipment supplier? I can assess whether the proposed scope addresses the underlying problem and explain possible alternatives. Not every replacement is unnecessary; the purpose is to compare options on technical and economic grounds before you commit.
