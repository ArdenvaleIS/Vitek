Common questions

Do I need to know which service to request?

No. Describe the symptoms or decision you face. Vitek will determine whether the issue fits his consulting scope.

Can you review a proposal before I purchase equipment?

Yes, when the subject is within the consulting scope. You can request an independent evaluation of the recommendation and possible alternatives.

When do we discuss costs?

After the issue is described and the likely scope is clearer. Vitek will reply with what the assessment could cost; no paid work starts without agreement.

Do you perform installations or licensed trade work?

No. Technical consulting and optimization do not include work that requires a California electrician, contractor, or professional engineer license.
