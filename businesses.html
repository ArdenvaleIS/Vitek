Independent consulting for businesses and organizations

Solve recurring problems. Improve how your systems work.

Office networks, connected equipment, industrial automation, building moisture or operational inefficiencies can have causes that are easy to miss when each component is examined in isolation.

I look across hardware, software, controls, operating practices and system interactions to identify likely causes and evaluate practical improvements. My recommendations are independent of equipment sales and replacement incentives.
