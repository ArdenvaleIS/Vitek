Independent consulting for businesses and organizations

Solve recurring problems. Improve how your systems work.

Recurring IT failures, network problems, production downtime, equipment integration issues or costly replacement proposals? These problems often involve interactions between systems.

I bring decades of hands-on IT deployment, configuration and troubleshooting experience, along with industrial automation and systems-analysis work. I can help resolve appropriate technical issues directly and independently evaluate larger investments.

Does this sound familiar?

“Our office network is slow or unreliable.”

I can diagnose, configure and troubleshoot business networks, Wi-Fi, computers and connected equipment, including remote support where suitable.

“A technical problem keeps coming back.”

I investigate recurring symptoms and related systems to identify causes that may be outside the component initially blamed.

“Production equipment causes downtime or inefficiency.”

I assess industrial robots, manufacturing automation and interactions among mechanics, controls, electronics and software.

“We are being told to replace or upgrade equipment.”

I can check whether the proposed equipment is actually causing the problem and evaluate optimization and alternatives before replacement.

“Our building has recurring moisture, condensation or musty odors.”

I can assess accessible building conditions, ventilation, HVAC and insulation factors that may contribute. See Mold & Moisture consulting.

“Our facilities consume too much energy.”

I can review building-system performance, energy use and operational improvement opportunities.

Independent technical advice before capital expenditure.

The system that appears to be failing may not be the actual cause of the problem. I investigate the wider process and evaluate whether repair, configuration, controls, operating changes or another component could resolve the issue before a major replacement is approved.
